# MetaMirror

**Top-player meta, mirrored to your character.**

MetaMirror shows you exactly what the best players of *your* spec are running — and holds it up against your own character, live. No browser tabs, no spreadsheets: open your character sheet and the meta is right there next to it.

Data is built from **Warcraft Logs** (what top parses actually use) and **bloodmallet.com** (trinket sim DPS). Your spec is auto-detected — nothing to configure.

---

## Features

### 📊 Live secondary-stat comparison
Every secondary (Haste, Crit, Mastery, Versatility) shown against the top-player target: a fill bar with a target marker, your live percentage, and the exact rating. Swap a piece of gear and the bars move instantly — you see at a glance what's below, on, or over target.

### 🛡️ Best-in-slot gear with its source
The meta piece for every slot, with **where it drops** pulled straight from the in-game Adventure Guide (boss & instance), how many of the top players use it, and a marker for what you already own. Crafted and tier-set pieces are labelled as such.

### 💎 Trinket tier list — split by content
Sim-DPS tiers from bloodmallet.com, **re-ordered by real top-player usage** for your content. Mythic+ and Raid are ranked separately, so the list actually differs between them instead of showing the same order twice.

### ✨ Enchants, gems & consumables
The exact enchants, gem colours and consumables (flask, food, augment rune) the top players run — copy-ready, no guesswork.

### 🔔 BiS-drop alert with a friendly whisper
When a top-tier item drops for someone in your group, MetaMirror flags it and offers a **one-click polite whisper** to ask for it — realm included, so it reaches the right person.

---

## Usage

- Open your **character sheet** — MetaMirror docks to the right of it automatically.
- Or type **`/mm`** to toggle the window anywhere.
- Switch between **M+** and **Raid** with the toggle in the header; the whole panel follows.

## Notes

- Built for **World of Warcraft: Midnight** (Interface 120100).
- Item sources are read from the Adventure Guide the first time you log in after a game patch, then cached — so there's no repeated loading hitch on later logins.
- Data from **Warcraft Logs** and **bloodmallet.com**.
